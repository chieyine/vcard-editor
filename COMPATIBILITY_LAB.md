| Profile | Fixture | Import/export test | Status |

| Apple Contacts / iCloud | synthetic vCard 3.0 | Not run on a live device | Guidance only |

The compatibility checker reports local file signals and deliberately does not claim that a destination will accept every field. A dated platform test must be recorded here before publishing a stronger compatibility claim.
